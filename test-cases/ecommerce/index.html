From Confusion to Complete Financial Clarity

The Customer

Our client was a growing e-commerce business owner from Raleigh, North Carolina, selling products across Amazon, Shopify, and Walmart. Despite strong sales growth, the business owner was unable to identify real profitability and was struggling to stay tax compliant across multiple states.

Processing thousands of orders per month across multiple platforms, the business faced mounting complexity in bookkeeping, inventory management, and tax reporting. Without accurate financial data, the owner could not identify which products were truly profitable, making pricing, marketing, and inventory decisions increasingly difficult.

The Challenges

The multi-platform nature of the business created compounding financial complexity across bookkeeping, tax compliance, inventory, and profitability analysis that overwhelmed manual processes.

  • Lack of true profitability insight: Platform fees, advertising costs, returns, and shipping were not tracked accurately, masking real margins at the product and channel level.
  • Sales tax compliance challenges: Multi-state nexus obligations created significant risk of penalties and complicated the reporting process across 15+ states.
  • High transaction volume: Thousands of monthly orders overwhelmed manual bookkeeping, creating errors, delays, and unreliable financial records.
  • Inventory mismatches: Differences between actual stock and accounting records caused incorrect COGS calculations, distorting profitability reporting.
  • No product-level analysis: Management could not determine which SKUs were profitable or loss-making, preventing informed pricing and assortment decisions.
  • Delayed reporting: Monthly reporting took weeks, preventing timely pricing, marketing, and inventory decisions at critical business moments.
The Solutions

We centralized data, automated processes, and implemented insights-driven analytics to reveal the true financial picture across all sales platforms and product lines.

  • Centralized accounting integration: Integrated all sales platforms and payment gateways into a single accounting system, automating transaction imports and eliminating manual entry.
  • SKU-level profitability reports: Built product-level profitability reports factoring in platform fees, advertising costs, shipping, and returns for each SKU.
  • Automated sales tax tracking: Implemented automated sales tax tracking and nexus monitoring, ensuring accurate filings and full compliance across all applicable states.
  • Automated inventory and COGS: Automated inventory and COGS updates, keeping financial records continuously aligned with real stock positions.
  • Monthly performance dashboards: Developed monthly dashboards highlighting profitability, customer acquisition cost, ROI, and product-level performance metrics.
The Result

The engagement delivered full financial clarity across platforms, eliminated compliance risk, and enabled data-driven decisions that directly increased revenue and margins.

  • Time savings: Saved over 30 hours per month on manual bookkeeping through automation and platform integration.
  • Net margin improvement: Net margins increased by 20% following the reallocation of budget toward profitable products and channels.
  • Tax compliance: Achieved full compliance across 15+ states, eliminating exposure to nexus penalties and filing risks.
  • Revenue increase: Revenue increased by 22% by focusing marketing and inventory investment on the most profitable product SKUs.
The Summary

The engagement transformed a fragmented, manually-managed multi-platform e-commerce operation into a centralized, automated, and insight-driven financial system. Product profitability became visible, tax compliance was achieved across all states, and strategic decisions driven by accurate data led to both revenue growth and margin improvement.

  • 30+ hours/month saved on bookkeeping
  • 20% net margin increase
  • Fully compliant in 15+ states
  • 22% revenue increase
